### Why each set is used

- **LPLC2** — Looming-selective visual projection neurons (radial-motion opponency); encode the angular SIZE of an approaching object and synapse directly onto the Giant Fiber. Driven by the looming transducer.
- **LC4** — Visual projection neurons encoding the angular VELOCITY of a looming object; direct input to the Giant Fiber. Driven by the looming transducer.
- **GF** — Giant Fiber descending neurons (DNp01), one per side. A single GF spike triggers the short-mode escape takeoff; in dino-fly a GF spike is the JUMP command. The table's neurotransmitter prediction for the GF is low-confidence and differs between sides (ACh 0.32 / Glu 0.27); we only read its spikes.
- **LPLC1** — Looming-responsive VPN without direct GF input; specificity control (same drive, wrong cell type).
- **LC6** — Looming-responsive VPN linked to takeoff when activated; specificity control and alternative pathway.
- **DNp02** — Descending neuron in the looming/takeoff neighbourhood of the GF; alternative readout candidate.
- **DNp04** — Descending neuron in the looming/takeoff neighbourhood of the GF; alternative readout candidate.
- **DNp06** — Descending neuron in the looming/takeoff neighbourhood of the GF; alternative readout candidate.
- **DNp11** — Descending neuron in the looming/takeoff neighbourhood of the GF; alternative readout candidate.
- **DN_ALL** — All annotated descending neurons: used to rank the GF's response to looming drive against every other possible motor readout (specificity analysis), never as a learned readout.
- **KC** — Kenyon cells of the mushroom body (Phase 4: presynaptic side of the only plastic synapses).
- **MBON** — Mushroom body output neurons (Phase 4: postsynaptic side of the plastic KC→MBON synapses).
- **PAM** — PAM-cluster dopaminergic neurons (reward teaching signal, Phase 4).
- **PPL1** — PPL1-cluster dopaminergic neurons (punishment teaching signal, Phase 4).
